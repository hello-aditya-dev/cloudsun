# CloudSun Dental — Product Specification

## Product positioning

CloudSun Dental is the AI-powered front desk for modern dental practices. It handles new-patient enquiries, appointment requests, missed calls, cancellations, recalls and treatment follow-up — while keeping the practice team in control.

## Target buyers

- Practice owners (revenue, full schedules, new patients, staffing costs)
- Practice managers (front-desk workload, scheduling, cancellations, no-shows)
- Front-desk coordinators (one inbox, clear priorities, patient context)
- Dental group operators (multi-location, standardised processes, central reporting)

## Core workflows

1. New-patient intake — capture, qualify, book
2. After-hours coverage — never miss an enquiry
3. Cancellation recovery — fill slots from waitlist
4. Recall and reactivation — bring due patients back
5. Treatment follow-up — follow up on unfinished treatment plans
6. Emergency screening — red-flag detection and escalation
7. Human handoff — clinical questions, angry patients, VIP patients

## Safety rules

- Emergency red flags trigger immediate escalation
- Clinical questions always hand off — never diagnoses
- No treatment outcome promises
- No invented pricing
- Respects patient consent and do-not-contact status

## Current status

Interactive product prototype. All features simulated. No real patient data. No production integrations.
