# CloudSun Dental — Pilot Measurement

## What CloudSun measures during a pilot

- Missed calls handled
- Enquiries captured
- Appointment requests
- Appointments booked
- Human handoffs
- Cancellations recovered
- Recall appointments
- Response time
- Staff time addressed
- Failed or corrected responses

## Pilot methodology

1. **Observe** current workflow
2. **Configure** approved practice information
3. **Test** scenarios in demo mode
4. **Controlled launch** in limited channels
5. **Require approval** for sensitive actions
6. **Review** every failed conversation
7. **Measure** outcomes
8. **Expand** only after quality targets are met
