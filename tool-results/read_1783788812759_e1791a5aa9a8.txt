   223→=== PAGE 6 ===
   224→A. Reveal
   225→For content entering the viewport:
   226→Opacity from 0 to 1.
   227→Vertical offset between 12px and 28px.
   228→Optional very subtle blur from 4px to 0.
   229→Duration between 0.45 and 0.65 seconds.
   230→Stagger between 50ms and 90ms.
   231→Run once for normal marketing sections.
   232→Do not make every paragraph independently animate.
   233→Animate logical content groups.
   234→B. Reflow
   235→For filters, tabs, cards, panels and changing data:
   236→Use layout.
   237→Use layoutId.
   238→Use LayoutGroup.
   239→Preserve spatial continuity.
   240→Avoid elements suddenly teleporting.
   241→C. Presence
   242→For drawers, modals, menus, context panels and conditional content:
   243→Use AnimatePresence.
   244→Animate both entrance and exit.
   245→Keep exit animation short.
   246→Do not leave invisible elements blocking pointer input.
   247→D. Feedback
   248→For hover , tap, selection, success and loading:
   249→Buttons depress slightly on tap.
   250→Cards lift subtly on hover .
   251→Selected navigation indicators glide.
   252→Success states confirm actions.
   253→Loading states feel responsive.
   254→Avoid constant pulsing where no action is occurring.
